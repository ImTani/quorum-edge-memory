Two people heard two dates. Quorum asks the owner.

Lakshya's email says “Sharma wedding edit”. Tanishk's note says “Sharma delivery”. Different words, same job.

Quorum compares what each note is about: the job and the kind of fact, never the value. Close enough, with different values, and both laptops open the same conflict on their own. Nothing is overwritten. The person who owns the task decides, and the decision reaches everyone.

Built on Qdrant Edge.

Each laptop runs its own Qdrant Edge shard. The team hub is a Qdrant server. No cloud calls in the private path.

Memory
Every claim is a point with three named vectors: text for meaning, key for what it's about, bm25 for keywords.
Search
Hybrid: BM25 and dense results fused with RRF, answered on the laptop in 7 to 70 ms.
Conflicts
A nearest-neighbour query on key, filtered to the same kind of fact and live claims, threshold 0.82. The same pair gets the same conflict id on every laptop.
Sync
An outbox that survives restarts pushes team claims to the hub; each laptop pulls what changed since its cursor.
Understanding
A local model turns emails, WhatsApp and notes into claims with sources. Dates are worked out in code, not by the model.
